*Insurance this week*

Cross-border deals dominate. Allianz has emerged as the frontrunner for HSBC's Singapore insurance unit at up to $2 billion, the government sold a 5% stake in GIC Re via OFS that was oversubscribed 3.72 times, and insurtech Turtlemint opened its ₹883-crore IPO after pulling ₹397 crore in anchor money.

*Major Business & Market Deals*

_Allianz Frontrunner for HSBC Life Singapore:_ Allianz SE has outpaced Sumitomo Life and Dai-ichi Life Group in the race to acquire HSBC's Singapore insurance unit, with HSBC seeking up to $2 billion. Talks are in late stages but other bidders, including Sun Life and Nippon Life, remain in the frame.

_Government Sells 5% of GIC Re:_ The Centre divested its full 5% (including the green-shoe option) in GIC Re via OFS at a floor price of ₹352 per share, a 9.1% discount to the previous close. The issue was oversubscribed 3.72 times by institutional investors, raising about ₹3,088 crore.

_Turtlemint IPO Opens:_ Insurtech platform Turtlemint Fintech Solutions kicked off its ₹883-crore IPO today, having raised ₹397.2 crore from 32 anchor investors at ₹152 per share. Bajaj Life, ICICI Prudential Life, and Axis Max Life are among the insurance-sector backers in the anchor book.

_NSE IPO Windfall for PSU Insurers:_ The upcoming NSE IPO will deliver outsized returns for early shareholders, including several PSU banks and insurers that picked up stakes at weighted-average costs below ₹1 per share. The legacy positions stand to be re-rated sharply at listing valuations.

*Regulatory Actions & Corporate Governance*

_IRDAI Sets Up AI Working Group:_ The regulator constituted a seven-member panel chaired by Sandeep Shukla (Director, IIIT Hyderabad) to draft governance frameworks, safeguards and an audit framework for AI use in insurance. Members include officials from CERT-In, RBI's IT arm, SBI Life, Star Health, and ICICI Lombard.

_RBI Norms May Reshape Bancassurance:_ The RBI's anti-mis-selling norms for banks are expected to redraw bancassurance business models and hit cross-sell revenues across the sector, layering on top of IRDAI's own crackdown on commission-led selling. Industry channels are bracing for distribution-economics churn.

*Industry & Premium Trends*

_Digit Life Posts 51% Premium Growth:_ Digit Life Insurance reported a 51% jump in FY26 premium income and settled ₹458 crore in claims, underscoring the speed at which newer digital-first life insurers are scaling. The result strengthens the IIHL group's narrative ahead of its planned insurance-arm listing.

_JM Financial: Star Health Top Pick:_ In a fresh sector note, JM Financial backed retail health as the dominant growth engine for non-life insurers and named Star Health and Allied as its top pick. The call reinforces the broader trend of capital tilting toward standalone health insurers.

_ICICI Lombard Clarifies E20 Fuel Rule:_ ICICI Lombard publicly clarified that running E20 ethanol-blended petrol in older vehicles will not automatically void motor-insurance coverage, after consumer confusion over recent fuel transitions. The insurer said claims will be assessed on actual cause-and-effect.
