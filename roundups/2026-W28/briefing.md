*Insurance this week*

The week belongs to regulation. IRDAI is moving to overhaul insurance commissions, a change experts say could curb mis-selling and rebuild customer trust. Alongside, private life insurers outpaced LIC as industry new business premium rose 16.6% in Q1, and June premiums stayed strong.

*Major Business & Market Deals*

_Private Life Insurers Outpace LIC:_ Private life insurers grew faster than LIC in Q1, driving industry new business premium up 16.6%. Their combined market share is now nearing 40%, a steady shift in the balance of the life insurance market.

_June Premiums Climb:_ Life insurers collected ₹46,490 crore in new business premium in June, up more than 13% year-on-year. General insurance premium rose 16% in the same month, with health insurers leading the growth.

_SBI Funds Pre-IPO Placement:_ SBI will sell a 1.42% stake in SBI Funds Management through a pre-IPO placement, setting up the asset manager's listing. The move lets SBI trim its holding and surface value ahead of the public issue.

_Aviva Buyout Follows FDI Roadmap:_ Aviva's full buyout of its India business follows the long-planned FDI roadmap that has steadily opened insurance to higher foreign ownership. The deal signals continued global-insurer appetite for wholly-owned Indian operations.

*Regulatory Actions & Corporate Governance*

_Commission Overhaul Proposed:_ IRDAI is likely to tighten commission norms as part of a wider overhaul. Experts say clearer rules could curb mis-selling and rebuild customer trust, though intermediaries face pressure on the payout-led economics that drive much of their income.

_Costs Beyond Commissions:_ IRDAI is now examining life insurers' costs beyond commissions, widening its expense-of-management scrutiny. The regulator wants a fuller view of where distribution money goes, signalling that the cost crackdown will not stop at agent payouts.

_Dark Patterns Crackdown:_ IRDAI is moving against "dark patterns", the manipulative design tricks that nudge customers into unwanted cover. The push aims to strengthen consumer trust and sits alongside the regulator's broader mis-selling and commission reforms.

_Bima Sugam and Reforms:_ IRDAI is accelerating reforms, including the Bima Sugam digital marketplace launch, a push for a public policy registry, and a health-sector overhaul. Together they point to a more transparent, digital-first insurance market.

*Industry & Premium Trends*

_Private Share Nears 40%:_ Private insurers powered life insurance premium growth in Q1, lifting their market share close to 40%. The momentum reflects faster product and distribution moves by private players compared with the state-owned incumbent.

_Health Insurers Lead Growth:_ General insurance premium rose 16% in June, with health insurers the clear engine of growth. Retail health continues to pull the non-life segment forward, extending a trend that has held for several quarters.

_RBI Flags Policy Surrenders:_ The RBI flagged rising surrenders of life insurance policies, a sign that some customers are exiting cover early. Higher surrenders can dent insurer persistency and point to gaps in how policies are sold and understood.

_Gen Z Enters Insurance:_ India's insurance sector is seeing a Gen Z takeover as younger buyers enter earlier, but the twist is they favour digital, low-ticket and flexible cover. Insurers must rethink products and distribution to hold this cohort.
