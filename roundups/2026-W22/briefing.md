# Industry Briefing — W22 2026 (29 May 2026)

**Insurance this week**

The biggest moves are structural and supervisory. The government has notified 100% FDI in insurance through the automatic route. IRDAI has tightened executive pay on two fronts — withholding variable pay for some CEOs over expense-of-management breaches, and redesigning the framework so half of senior assessment is now linked to claims and customer outcomes. And DIPAM has flagged a ₹10,000-crore LIC stake sale for next month, even as LIC's maiden 1:1 bonus issue takes its record date today.

**Major Business & Market Deals**

*100% FDI Notified:* The May 2 FEMA NDI amendment allows 100% FDI in insurance via the automatic route, with intermediaries included. LIC stays capped at 20%, and insurers with foreign investment must keep a resident Indian as chair, MD or CEO.

*₹10,000-crore LIC Stake Sale:* DIPAM is set to begin formal marketing in late June or early July for a 1.5–2% LIC stake worth up to ₹10,000 crore (~$1 billion). Goldman Sachs, Motilal Oswal, BNP Paribas and IIFL Capital are on the deal; LIC shares fell ~4% on the report.

*LIC Bonus Record Date Today:* Separately, LIC's first-ever 1:1 bonus share issue takes Friday, May 29 as its record date — shareholders on books today get the bonus.

**Regulatory Actions & Corporate Governance**

*Variable Pay Withheld:* IRDAI has withheld the performance-linked pay of insurance CEOs whose companies missed Expense of Management targets or strayed from their EoM glide paths. The action follows reviews of FY25 results and early FY26 trends.

*Pay Tied to Customer Outcomes:* A revised KMP framework now links 50% of senior assessment — for MDs, CEOs and key personnel — to financial soundness, product performance, claims responsiveness, grievance redressal, Ind AS implementation and removing "dark patterns" in customer journeys. Effective FY27. Insurers have flagged concerns about regulatory overreach into operational matters.

*Uniform Claim Definition Coming:* IRDAI has directed non-life insurers to agree on a single definition of a "claim" and a uniform methodology for claim settlement ratios. Today insurers count differently — some at filing, others only after liability verification — and rejected or disputed claims sometimes still show up as "settled". The General Insurance Council has submitted its views.

*Health Moratorium Cut to Five Years:* The period after which health claims cannot be rejected for non-disclosure or misrepresentation has been reduced from 8 years to 5. Proven fraud and permanent policy exclusions remain valid rejection grounds.

**Industry & Premium Trends**

*Niva Bupa Eyes Top Two:* India's third-largest standalone health insurer reported FY26 GWP of ₹9,432.9 crore, with retail health contributing ~70% (₹6,581.5 crore) and growing 35% YoY. It now targets 22–24% retail CAGR — 6–7 percentage points above the industry — over the next 3–4 years. Claims ratio rose to 64.9% from 63.8%, opening the door to high single-digit premium hikes.

*Insurance Cyber-Fraud Cases Rise:* Multiple insurance-themed cyber-fraud cases surfaced across Navi Mumbai this week, including a Vashi resident duped of ₹61.49 lakh in a fake-refund scam and an IT manager duped of ₹7 lakh in a fake life-policy scam. Police are tracking organised rings.
