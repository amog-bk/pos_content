*Insurance this week*

The Centre reshuffled IRDAI's board, appointing the New India Assurance chief and LIC's MD as members while extending two others, even as SBI's chairman confirmed an IPO is on the cards for SBI General Insurance. MetLife is also reportedly chasing a majority stake in PNB MetLife.

*Major Business & Market Deals*

_IRDAI Board Restructure:_ The government appointed Girija Subramanian (CMD, New India Assurance) as Member-Distribution and Dinesh Pant (MD, LIC) as Member-Actuary on IRDAI's board. The tenures of Deepak Sood (Non-life) and Rajay Sinha (Finance & Investment) were extended by two years, till the age of 65.

_SBI General Insurance IPO Confirmed:_ SBI Chairman C.S. Setty confirmed plans to list both SBI General Insurance and SBI Mutual Fund. The SBI MF IPO is targeted within 12 months; no schedule yet for SBI General. It would be the bank's third major subsidiary listing after SBI Cards and SBI Life.

_MetLife Eyes Majority in PNB MetLife:_ Mint reports US insurer MetLife is keen to raise its stake to a majority in its Indian life-insurance joint venture with Punjab National Bank, where it currently holds about 47%. A deal would deepen foreign control under the new 100% FDI regime.

_CCI Clears Sanlam-Shriram Deal:_ The Competition Commission approved South Africa-based Sanlam Emerging Markets' acquisition of a 2.8% stake in Shriram Life Insurance via a preferential share issue, deepening the Sanlam Group's long-standing strategic partnership across Shriram's Indian financial-services businesses.

_Saudi Re Opens GIFT City Branch:_ Saudi Re inaugurated its IIO-GIFT City branch, its second Asian foothold after Malaysia, and named Ruchi Jain, ex-Swiss Re VP, as Principal Officer. The move follows IRDAI norms favouring reinsurers with local branches, as ADNIC, Santam and PartnerRe also build GIFT presence.

*Regulatory Actions & Corporate Governance*

_Written Reasons Mandatory for Claim Rejections:_ IRDAI is pressing insurers to give policyholders specific written reasons for rejecting health-insurance claims, citing the relevant policy clauses. The push comes against a steep rise in Bima Bharosa grievances: 47,658 in FY24, 64,365 in FY25, and 73,729 by February in FY26.

_Aviva CEO: Mis-Selling Is the Real Problem:_ Aviva Life's Asit Rath argues mis-selling, not claims, is life insurance's biggest challenge. Tighter IRDAI rules, he says, are pushing the industry away from commission-led selling toward life-stage advice, data-driven underwriting and long-term customer engagement.

*Industry & Premium Trends*

_HDFC Life Settles 99.7% of Claims:_ HDFC Life reported a 99.7% individual death-claim settlement ratio for FY26 (99.8% overall), crediting AI-driven Zero-Touch Processing and fast-track handling. Q4 net profit rose 4% to ₹497 crore with value of new business up 7%.

_ICICI Lombard Flags 'Implant' Fraud:_ An Assam Special Investigation Team found that a 2015 road-accident case used to claim insurance from ICICI Lombard involved a planted motorcycle. The case underscores rising organised motor-insurance fraud and reinforces the regulator's growing focus on claims integrity.

_Liberty General Enters Cyber Cover:_ Liberty General Insurance launched a cyber-insurance suite for the Indian market, targeting individuals and businesses against ransomware, data breaches and online fraud. It joins a growing field as cyber risk and IRDAI's cybersecurity focus sharpen demand for digital-risk protection.
