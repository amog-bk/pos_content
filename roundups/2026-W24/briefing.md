*Insurance this week*

M&A is the theme this week — MetLife is reportedly seeking a majority in PNB MetLife, and the CCI cleared Sanlam's stake purchase in Shriram Life, even as the LIC stake sale nears. Aviva's CEO, meanwhile, calls mis-selling — not claims — life insurance's biggest problem.

*Major Business & Market Deals*

_MetLife Eyes Majority in PNB MetLife:_ Mint reports US insurer MetLife is keen to raise its stake to a majority in its Indian life-insurance joint venture with Punjab National Bank, where it currently holds about 47%. A deal would deepen foreign control under the new 100% FDI regime.

_CCI Clears Sanlam–Shriram Deal:_ The Competition Commission approved South Africa-based Sanlam Emerging Markets' acquisition of a 2.8% stake in Shriram Life Insurance via a preferential share issue, deepening the Sanlam Group's long-standing strategic partnership across Shriram's Indian financial-services businesses.

_LIC Stake Sale Imminent:_ The government's sale of ~2% of LIC — worth up to ₹10,000 crore ($1 billion) — is expected to launch this month via institutional investors, marking its first major sell-down since the 2022 listing. Goldman Sachs, BNP Paribas and IIFL are managing it.

*Regulatory Actions & Corporate Governance*

_Aviva CEO: Mis-Selling Is the Real Problem:_ Aviva Life's Asit Rath argues mis-selling, not claims, is life insurance's biggest challenge — and tighter IRDAI rules are pushing the industry away from commission-led selling toward life-stage advice, data-driven underwriting and long-term customer engagement.

_Insurers' Investments Top ₹74 Lakh Crore:_ IRDAI data shows the industry's investment portfolio reached ₹74.4 lakh crore by March 2025, up 10.1%, with life insurers managing 91% of assets. Life insurers' equity allocation rose around 20%, reflecting deeper participation in Indian capital markets.

*Industry & Premium Trends*

_HDFC Life Settles 99.7% of Claims:_ HDFC Life reported a 99.7% individual death-claim settlement ratio for FY26 (99.8% overall), crediting AI-driven Zero-Touch Processing and fast-track handling. Q4 net profit rose 4% to ₹497 crore with value of new business up 7%.

_Liberty General Enters Cyber Cover:_ Liberty General Insurance launched a cyber-insurance suite for the Indian market, targeting individuals and businesses against ransomware, data breaches and online fraud — joining a growing field as cyber risk and IRDAI's cybersecurity focus sharpen demand for digital-risk protection.
