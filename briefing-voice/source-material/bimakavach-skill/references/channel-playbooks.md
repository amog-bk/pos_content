# BimaKavach — Channel Playbooks

## CHANNEL 1: EMAIL

### Email Philosophy
Every email BimaKavach sends to a client or prospect should feel like it came from a knowledgeable colleague, not a marketing system. The tone is that of a named advisor — warm, specific, and worth reading.

---

### Email Structure Rules

**Word count:** 250 words maximum per email (body only, excluding subject/preview). This is a hard cap. Shorter is almost always better.

**Named advisor voice:** Emails are written as if from a specific person (e.g., Tejas Jain, or the assigned relationship manager), not from "BimaKavach." Sign off with a real name.

**Subject line:** Clear and specific. No clickbait. Lead with the benefit or the question the reader is already asking.

**Preview text:** Treat as a second subject line — it must add information, not repeat the subject.

---

### Email Drip Cadence

BimaKavach uses a **5-touch drip sequence** for nurture campaigns (e.g., GHI cross-sell, Niramaya app):

| Email | Day | Purpose | Temperature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | 0 | Welcome / introduce the topic | Warm |
| Day 4 | 4 | Educate — one specific benefit/insight | Warm |
| Day 9 | 9 | Social proof / client scenario | Warm-Hot |
| Day 14 | 14 | Address objection or FAQ | Warm-Hot |
| Day 20 | 20 | Soft CTA / next step | Hot |

Do not send CTAs in Day 0 or Day 4 emails. Earn the right first.

---

### Hook Bank (Email Openers)

These are proven opener patterns for BimaKavach emails. Use or adapt:

- **The Business Scenario Hook:** "A 200-person logistics company in Pune recently had a worker injured on-site. Their WC policy covered the claim in full. Here's what made the difference…"
- **The Calendar Hook:** "With the new financial year underway, most CFOs are reviewing their insurance programmes…"
- **The Consequence Hook:** "One wrong board decision. One disgruntled shareholder. One regulatory notice. This is when D&O Insurance earns its premium."
- **The Question Hook:** "Is your group health policy actually covering what your employees think it covers?"
- **The Stat Hook:** "68% of Indian SMEs have never filed an insurance claim — not because they don't face risks, but because they don't know what they're covered for."
- **The Founder/Advisor Hook (Tejas voice):** "In four decades of insurance, my family has seen one thing remain constant: businesses that plan for risk sleep better than those that don't."

---

### Email Don'ts

- No "Dear Sir/Madam" — use the recipient's first name
- No "Please find attached herewith" — just attach and reference it naturally
- No "Hope this email finds you well" — delete it
- No walls of text — use one idea per paragraph
- No ALL CAPS for emphasis — use bold sparingly instead
- No vague CTAs like "Click here" — be specific: "Book a 15-minute call", "Download the product summary"

---

### Founder's Note (Tejas Jain) — Special Rules

When writing in Tejas's voice (gifting campaigns, year-end notes, major announcements):

1. **Preserve his phrasing** — if he has reviewed and approved a draft, do not change his words without consent.
2. **Conversational but dignified** — warm without being casual. He speaks as a professional with deep roots in the industry.
3. **Personal references welcome** — references to family legacy, long-standing clients, or industry milestones fit his voice naturally.
4. **No marketing language** — no "best-in-class", no "exciting offer", no "limited time."

## CHANNEL 2: WHATSAPP (POSP / Agent Community)

### Community Context
BimaKavach manages a WhatsApp community of POSP (Point of Salesperson) partners — licensed insurance sellers who distribute BimaKavach products. This is a key growth channel and requires consistent, high-quality engagement.

The community is split into **zone-specific groups** (e.g., North, South, West, etc.).

---

### WhatsApp Community Message Rules

**Format:**
- Messages are short, scannable, and designed for mobile.
- Use line breaks generously — no dense paragraphs.
- Bold key terms using *asterisks* (WhatsApp markdown).
- Use emojis purposefully — 1–3 per message maximum. Not decorative noise.
- End every message with a clear call to action or engagement prompt.

**Tone:**
- Community-first. These are partners, not prospects.
- Appreciate, educate, and celebrate — don't just broadcast.
- Speak to their interests: commissions, skill-building, product knowledge, recognition.

**Content mix (monthly):**
- Product education (what it is, who needs it, how to pitch)
- Regulatory/compliance updates (IRDAI news, new rules)
- Webinar invites and follow-ups
- Recognition posts (top performers, milestones)
- Motivational or seasonal posts
- Campaign launches and sales support material

---

### WhatsApp Message Structure (Standard)

```
[Hook line — 1 sentence, bold if needed]

[Context / body — 2–4 lines max]

[CTA or engagement prompt]

— BimaKavach Team
```

---

### WhatsApp Webinar Sequence (Standard Flow)

For any webinar/event targeting the POSP community:

| Message | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement | 7 days before | Introduce topic, build interest |
| Education post | 4–5 days before | Give a teaser insight on the topic |
| Reminder 1 | 2 days before | Date/time confirmation, registration link |
| Reminder 2 | Morning of event | Final nudge, "join today" energy |
| Live nudge | 15 min before start | "We're going live!" |
| Reschedule notice | If needed | Apologise briefly, give new date, reaffirm value |
| Follow-up / recap | Day after | Key takeaways, recording link if available |

---

### WhatsApp Client Utility Templates (Meta Compliance)

For WhatsApp messages sent **directly to clients** (not the agent community), BimaKavach uses **pre-approved Meta utility templates** only.

Rules:
- Template category must be **Utility** (transactional/service-related), not Marketing.
- Avoid promotional language that could trigger Meta reclassification.
- Templates must be submitted and approved before use.
- Message must relate to an existing business relationship or service action (e.g., policy renewal reminder, claim status update, document request).

Framing patterns that pass utility classification:
- "Your policy [number] is due for renewal on [date]. Here are your options…"
- "We've received your claim request. Here's what happens next…"
- "As requested, here is your policy summary for [product]…"

## CHANNEL 3: BLOG / SEO CONTENT

### Blog Content Principles

- **E-E-A-T oriented** — demonstrate Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness in every post.
- **Industry-vertical targeting** — write for specific sectors (logistics, manufacturing, IT, healthcare) rather than generic SME audiences.
- **Risk-scenario hooks** — open with a real or hypothetical business risk scenario, then introduce the insurance solution.
- **Topical authority** — BimaKavach aims to own the content space for Indian commercial insurance. Blog topics cluster around product lines (WC, CGL, D&O, etc.) and regulatory themes (OSH Code, DPDP Act).
- **SEO tooling:** Ahrefs Lite is used for keyword research. Rank tracking via Google Apps Script + Google Sheets.

**Blog topic generation approach:** Start with a target industry vertical (e.g., logistics, FMCG, tech startups), identify the top 2–3 risks they face, then map each to a BimaKavach product. The blog answers the question a CFO or HR Head at that company would actually search.

## CHANNEL 4: SOCIAL MEDIA

- Tone: Concise, insight-driven, never promotional.
- Format: Short posts with one clear takeaway.
- Content sourced from blog posts, regulatory updates, campaign launches, or client case studies (anonymised).
- Visual assets: Navy + Gold palette, consistent with brand guidelines.
