# BimaKavach — Full Product Portfolio

BimaKavach sells exclusively **commercial/business insurance**. All products below are B2B.

---

## LIABILITY INSURANCE

### Directors & Officers (D&O) Insurance
Protects company directors and officers from personal liability arising from decisions made in their official capacity. Key audience: CFOs, Company Secretaries, promoters of listed and unlisted companies. Often triggered by: investor pressure, board changes, IPO preparation, regulatory action.
- Campaign angle used: "One wrong decision shouldn't cost a director their personal assets."
- Hosted webinar by Mr. Dhruvil Morakhiya for POSP education.

### Professional Indemnity (PI) Insurance
Covers professionals against claims of negligence, errors, or omissions in their professional services. Relevant for: IT companies, consultants, architects, engineers, medical professionals.
- Also known as Errors & Omissions (E&O) in some contexts.
- Lead reactivation campaign developed using Meta WhatsApp utility templates.

### Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance
Subset/variant of Professional Indemnity. Used interchangeably in some sectors.

### Commercial General Liability (CGL) Insurance
Covers third-party bodily injury and property damage claims arising from business operations, premises, or products. Broad coverage for any business with physical premises or customer interactions.
- Blog content developed targeting industry verticals.

### Product Liability Insurance
Covers manufacturers, distributors, and retailers against claims arising from defective products that cause injury or damage. Key audience: FMCG, electronics, pharma, auto components.
- Blog content developed with SEO orientation.

### Public Liability Insurance
Covers claims by third parties (members of the public) for injury or property damage caused by business activities. Often required by law for certain industries.

### Cyber Insurance
Covers losses from data breaches, cyberattacks, ransomware, and digital liability. Growing importance post-DPDP Act 2023. Audience: IT companies, fintech, e-commerce, healthcare, any data-heavy business.

### Crime Insurance
Covers financial losses from employee dishonesty, theft, fraud, forgery, and related crimes. Audience: banks, NBFCs, retail chains, logistics companies.

## PROPERTY & ENGINEERING INSURANCE

### Fire Insurance
Covers physical assets (buildings, machinery, stock) against fire and allied perils. Often the baseline policy for any business with physical assets.

### Fire Loss of Profit (FLOP) Insurance
Business interruption coverage linked to fire damage — compensates for lost profits during the reinstatement period.

### Machinery Breakdown (MB) Insurance
Covers repair/replacement costs when machinery breaks down due to mechanical or electrical failure. Audience: manufacturing, processing, heavy industry.

### Machinery Loss of Profit (MLOP) Insurance
Business interruption coverage linked to machinery breakdown.

### Office Package Insurance
Bundled policy covering office contents, electronics, glass, public liability, and more. Ideal for SMEs and service businesses. Often used as an entry-point product.

### Contractors All Risk (CAR) Insurance
Covers construction projects against damage to the works, third-party liability, and contractor's plant. Audience: real estate developers, infrastructure companies, EPC contractors.

### Erection All Risk (EAR) Insurance
Similar to CAR but for the erection of plant and machinery. Audience: energy, heavy engineering, industrial projects.

### Contractors Plant & Machinery (CPM) Insurance
Covers construction equipment and machinery against accidental damage. Audience: contractors, infrastructure companies.

## MARINE INSURANCE

### Marine Insurance (General)
Covers goods in transit — by sea, air, road, or rail — against physical loss or damage.

### Single Transit Policy
One-time coverage for a single consignment. Suitable for occasional shippers.

### Marine Open Policy (MOP)
Blanket coverage for all consignments over a period. Preferred by regular importers/exporters.

### Sales Turnover Policy
Marine coverage based on annual sales turnover — simpler and often more cost-effective for businesses with high shipment volumes.

## Notes for Content Writers

- Never refer to personal lines products (motor, home, health for individuals) — BimaKavach is strictly B2B.
- When writing product content, lead with the **business risk** being covered, not the policy mechanics.
- Use plain language — most SME decision-makers are not insurance experts.
- Always connect the product to a **real business scenario** the reader can recognise.
- For regulated communications (WhatsApp utility templates), ensure category framing is utility-first (not marketing) to avoid Meta reclassification.
