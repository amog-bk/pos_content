## Voice Pillars

### 1. Warm, Not Salesy
We never push. We educate, advise, and let the business case speak. The tone feels like a trusted colleague in the insurance world, not a sales executive.

- ✅ "Here's what a typical WC claim looks like for a logistics company — and how a well-structured policy would have covered it."
- ❌ "Get the BEST WC Insurance deal TODAY! Limited offer!"

### 2. Benefit-First, Mechanics-Second
Lead with the business outcome, not the policy feature. Decision-makers care about risk, cost, and compliance — not policy sub-clauses.

- ✅ "If a machine breaks down on a busy production day, Machinery Breakdown Insurance covers repair costs so your cash flow doesn't take the hit."
- ❌ "Machinery Breakdown Insurance covers sudden and accidental breakdown of mechanical and electrical equipment as per the policy schedule."

### 3. Credibility-Anchored
Reference real clients (Godrej, GAIL, Daikin, Panasonic) where relevant. Cite Tejas's fourth-generation expertise. Use data and scenarios to demonstrate depth of knowledge.

### 4. Metaphor-Laced (Selectively)
BimaKavach content — especially newsletters and long-form — uses metaphors to make abstract insurance concepts tangible. Use sparingly and purposefully.
- Example hook: "Think of D&O Insurance as a seatbelt for your boardroom."
- Example opener: "Every new financial year is a blank ledger…"

### 5. Reassuring, Not Fear-Mongering
We acknowledge risks matter, but we don't weaponise fear. The tone is calm, confident, and solution-oriented.

## Language Rules

1. **Write in Indian English** — not American or British. E.g., "financial year", "crore/lakh" if relevant, "Registered with IRDAI."
2. **Avoid jargon dumps** — explain terms when first used.
3. **Active voice** — always.
4. **Short paragraphs** — max 3 sentences in digital content.
5. **No exclamation mark abuse** — one per message maximum. Use sparingly in formal content.
6. **No filler phrases** — delete "We are pleased to inform you", "As per our records", "Kindly note."
7. **Numbers as digits** — "5 employees", not "five employees" in benefit-led body copy.
8. **Product names are proper nouns** — "Group Health Insurance", "D&O Insurance" (capitalised).

## What BimaKavach Is NOT

- Not a retail/personal insurance brand (no motor, home, travel for individuals)
- Not a discount aggregator ("cheapest rates")
- Not a faceless portal — we are relationship-driven
- Not alarmist — we solve problems, we don't amplify fear

## Content Temperature Framework

BimaKavach content operates on a temperature scale:

| Temperature | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold** | Pure information, no sell | Blog explainers, FAQ pages, compliance guides |
| **Warm** | Education with soft positioning | Newsletter, drip email Day 0, community posts |
| **Hot** | Clear CTA, conversion-oriented | Drip email Day 14/20, webinar follow-up, retargeting |

Never start cold-to-warm campaigns with hot content. Earn the right to the CTA.
