# BimaKavach — Company Context

## Who We Are

BimaKavach is an **IRDAI-registered direct insurance broker** headquartered in India. We specialise in commercial and business insurance for SMEs, mid-market companies, and large enterprises. We are not a retail/personal lines broker — every product we sell is business-facing.

**Founded by:** Tejas Jain, a fourth-generation insurance professional. His deep industry lineage gives BimaKavach significant credibility and institutional knowledge that competitors cannot replicate.

**Insurer network:** 25+ partner insurers, enabling multi-insurer comparison and best-fit placement for clients.

## Business Model

- **Direct broker model:** BimaKavach represents the client's interest, not the insurer's.
- **Revenue:** Commission-based from insurer placements.
- **Distribution:** Direct (online, inbound) + partner channel (POSP/agent network).
- **POSP/Agent community:** A WhatsApp-based community of Point of Salesperson partners (licensed insurance sellers). This community is a key growth lever and requires regular engagement, education, and recognition.

## Regulatory Context

- **IRDAI:** Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India — the governing body. BimaKavach is fully registered and compliant.
- **DPDP Act 2023:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act — BimaKavach operates as a data fiduciary. Compliance is an active consideration in all digital communications and data handling.
- **OSH Code 2020:** Occupational Safety and Health — used as a campaign hook, particularly for GHI drip sequences targeting HR audiences.
- **Meta WhatsApp compliance:** All WhatsApp messages to clients must use IRDAI-compliant utility template categories. Marketing templates face reclassification risk and must be structured carefully.
